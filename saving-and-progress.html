Keep your progress, energy and tools

Progress and supplies do not all live in the same place. Read this before moving to another browser or device, clearing browsing data, or transferring from the original address.

Guest play stays in this browser

You can play without signing in. Completed repairs, chapters, stars and read story entries are saved locally. Return using the same address, browser and browser profile to find that save. A private window has separate storage, which may disappear when it closes. An unfinished puzzle board is not restored after refreshing.

Google sign-in carries cloud progress

In Settings, sign in with Google to save account progress online. Use the same Google account on another device. The game shows whether progress is saved online or only locally while the cloud is unavailable. Signing in is separate on the original domain and game. If you first played as a guest, the settings offer an explicit import of that browser’s guest progress into the account.

Energy and tools remain local

Google sign-in does not synchronise energy or tool stock between devices. There are up to five energy charges. The first valid action in a puzzle reserves one; winning returns it. Losing, leaving after an action or refreshing a started attempt keeps it spent. Before the first ship departure, one charge returns every 10 minutes; afterwards, every 30 minutes. The in-game countdown shows your next recharge.

Moving from the original address to game.

Use Transfer my saved game before starting a new save on game. Open the original saved game in the browser where you used to play, send the copy and confirm the import on the new address. This includes local energy and tools as well as saved progress. The original copy is kept. Then continue to the game and sign in again with the same Google account if you used one before. Guests can continue without signing in.

If the popup is blocked, the transfer pages also offer a downloadable JSON backup and file import. Close other game tabs on the destination before importing. Review any conflicting saved data before choosing which copy to keep. Repeating the same transfer does not refill spent energy or tools.

If progress seems to be missing

Check the address, browser profile and Google account first. Do not reset the game or clear storage while looking for an older save. For missing local supplies after moving domains, use the original-save transfer rather than signing in repeatedly. If the original browser data has already been deleted, its local energy and tools cannot be recovered from Google cloud progress. Contact support with the affected device and account email; never send authentication tokens.

Statistics are your choice

The first visit offers a choice about sharing gameplay statistics. You can change it later in Settings, and declining does not limit play or cloud saving. Advertising privacy choices are separate. The privacy page explains the stored information, providers and deletion options.
