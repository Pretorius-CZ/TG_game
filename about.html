BEYOND THE SIGNAL

A small repair. A much bigger journey.

Beyond the Signal is a free sci-fi match-3 adventure. Every completed puzzle restores something in the world: a damaged ship, an abandoned installation or a place that still has a future.

Follow a signal that should not be there

You are the pilot of a supply ship. A beacon from a lost expedition draws you off course; an emergency landing leaves your ship badly damaged. The first task is simple: get one system working again. Repairs uncover the ship’s log and gradually turn survival into a journey after the signal.

The released adventure contains 200 levels. It begins aboard your ship and continues through distant worlds, stations and the traces of the missing expedition. You can read the optional story entries at your own pace; solving the puzzles moves the journey forward.

Puzzles with a visible result

Match neighbouring tiles to meet the goals within a move limit. Later puzzles introduce protective covers, resonators and charges that can clear several tiles at once. The reward is a direct change to the scene around you. Repairs do not require a separate currency or a resource shop.

Winning is enough to continue. Three-star results are an optional challenge, and completed puzzles can be revisited through Chapters. The game runs in mobile and desktop browsers without installation. On touch phones, play in portrait orientation.

An independent game, still growing

Beyond the Signal is created and operated by Michal Malich. Updates and player feedback help shape the adventure, its readability and its puzzle balance. The updates page records released changes; guides explain the current rules. For a problem report, include your device, browser, language and the level or place where it happened.
