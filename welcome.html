YOUR FLIGHT MANUAL

A little help along the way.

01 / Your first puzzle

Swap neighbouring tiles to match at least three of the same type. Check the goals above the board: some levels need specific colours, covers or charged rings. Complete every goal before running out of moves.

Winning unlocks the next task. Three stars are an optional challenge; you can replay completed repairs through Chapters.

02 / Pulse, Nova & tools

Match four in a line or five in a T or L to create Pulse, which hits a 3×3 area. Five or more in a line create Nova, which hits a row and column. Tap a charge to fire it for one move; chain reactions are free.

Tools below the board spend stock instead of moves. Laser hits one tile, Shuffle rearranges tiles, Swap exchanges neighbours, Beam hits a row and EMP hits a 3×3 area. Use ⓘ in a puzzle to reopen the illustrated help.

03 / Covers & resonators

A covered tile cannot be moved. Include it in a match to break its cover; the tile stays for another match. Blasts can also break covers.

Charge rings by making matches directly beside them: above, below, left or right. Each matching wave adds one charge per ring. Diagonal matches and direct blasts do not charge rings.

04 / Energy & taking a break

The first valid move reserves one energy charge. Winning returns it; losing, leaving after a move or refreshing keeps the charge spent. Opening a puzzle without making a move costs no energy. The energy display shows when a charge will return.

05 / Save your journey

Guest progress is saved in this browser. Sign in with Google for cloud progress on the same account across devices. Energy and tool stock are stored locally.

Played on the original address? Transfer your saved game first, then sign in with the same Google account on the new address. Signing in alone does not transfer local energy and tools. The original save remains available.
