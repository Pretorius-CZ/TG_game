BEYOND THE SIGNAL

Updates from beyond the signal

Follow released changes and clearly marked development news: new places to restore, clearer controls and improvements informed by player feedback. Features still in development are described separately from updates you can play today.

Oct 26, 2026
NEW LANGUAGE

Play all 200 levels in German.

German is now available alongside English and Czech, covering all 200 levels, objectives, controls, story and ship log. Choose Deutsch in the game settings; your saved progress and stars stay with you. A desktop test has completed all 200 levels, and mobile testing is continuing. Already playing in German? Tell us if a phrase feels unclear or a longer label does not fit.

Oct 23, 2026
GAME UPDATE

A tighter challenge, with room to try again.

We have reduced move limits in 33 selected levels from level 20 onwards, including several HARD challenges. Completed ship sections can be replayed before departure, so you can practise and improve your stars. Undo now prepares your available use first; a second tap returns the move. Your progress and earned stars are preserved.

Oct 23, 2026
IN DEVELOPMENT

The Android app is being tested.

An Android version of Beyond the Signal is now in internal testing on a real phone. We are checking controls, saved progress and rewarded ads before a wider release. The app is not yet publicly available to download or on Google Play. You can keep playing all 200 levels in your browser.

Oct 18, 2026
NEW CHAPTER

The journey reaches level 200.

The Answer adds thirteen puzzles across three beacon links and the central rescue station. Restore the connections and bring this journey after the signal to its conclusion. The adventure now includes 200 levels.

Oct 17, 2026
GAME UPDATE

More room for your next move.

Puzzle boards now make better use of small phones and larger displays. Result screens and controls are clearer, and touch phones show a rotate prompt when held sideways. Later levels have received targeted difficulty adjustments.

Oct 14, 2026
NEW CHAPTER

The Archive of Echoes awaits.

The story continues through the Arrival Dock and the Archive of Echoes. Explore four archive areas, restore their systems and uncover the history behind the signal. The released adventure now includes 187 levels.

Oct 09, 2026
GAME UPDATE

Clearer words. A smoother journey.

English and Czech text has been reviewed throughout the game. Puzzle tips and instructions are clearer, with improved readability on small screens. Level difficulty is unchanged.

Oct 09, 2026
PLAYTEST

A fresh pair of eyes on the adventure.

Our first Bounty Board playtest is underway. New players are exploring the opening, trying the puzzles, and sharing honest feedback to help us improve the experience.
