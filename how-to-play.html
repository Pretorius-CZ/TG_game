BEYOND THE SIGNAL

Your first moves in Beyond the Signal

You do not need to memorise every mechanic before you begin. Start with the goals above the board, then look for a useful match. This guide explains the rules that stay with you throughout the journey.

1. Swap two neighbours

Tap or click one tile and then a neighbouring tile, or drag it towards its neighbour. Ordinary tiles swap when the move creates a horizontal or vertical line of at least three identical tiles. A diagonal line does not count. An invalid swap does not use a move. Wait for the falling tiles and any cascades to settle before choosing the next action.

2. Read every goal above the board

Some puzzles ask for a total number of tiles; others need particular colours or several goals at once. A match in another colour can still rearrange the board, but it will not complete a colour goal. If the level includes covers or resonators, those also need to be finished. Empty spaces in a shaped board are not tiles.

3. Make the limited moves count

A valid swap or a tap that fires a charge uses one move. Cascades and chained blasts do not cost additional moves. Tools below the board use their own stock instead of a move. The final move is evaluated after its cascades finish, so a last-move success still wins the puzzle.

4. A win opens the way; stars are optional

Complete all goals to unlock the next task and its repair. Stars reflect how many moves remain from the original limit: at least 30% for three stars, at least 15% for two, otherwise one. Thresholds round up to whole moves; bonus moves do not increase this rating. Open Chapters, visit the room and choose a completed repair to replay it. Your best star result is kept.

When the next move is hard to see

Each attempt offers one free hint. Use the ⓘ button to reopen illustrated explanations of the mechanics and tools. If a board has no legal action, the game attempts to restore a playable arrangement without spending a move. Before spending a tool, check which goal is actually holding you back.
