Reporting a problem

Please include your device, browser, game language, level or location, and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send passwords, sign-in links or authentication tokens.

Request deletion of your Beyond the Signal account

Email us from the address used for Google sign-in where possible. Say that you want to delete your Beyond the Signal account and cloud progress. We verify ownership before deleting anything and confirm when the request is completed.

Send an account deletion request

This opens an email draft. Sending it requests deletion; it does not immediately delete your account. You can also write directly to flambiz.cz@gmail.com.

Deletion removes your game sign-in account and its cloud progress, stars and read log entries. It does not delete your Google account. Restarting the game or signing out is not account deletion.

Device-only saves, energy and tools remain on devices where they were stored. Clear them on each device if you also want those local copies removed.

Optional statistics use separate random browser identifiers. To delete them, use “Turn off and delete my statistics” in Settings on each device, or mention the request in your email so we can help identify the records. Never send passwords, sign-in links or session tokens.

Account and data requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data or account. For an account request, use the email linked to your Google sign-in where possible. We may need to verify ownership. Restarting the game resets progress; it does not delete your sign-in account or all statistics.
