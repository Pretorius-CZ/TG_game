Reporting a problem

Please include your device, browser, game language, level or location, and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send passwords, sign-in links or authentication tokens.

Account and data requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data or account. For an account request, use the email linked to your Google sign-in where possible. We may need to verify ownership. Restarting the game resets progress; it does not delete your sign-in account or all statistics.
