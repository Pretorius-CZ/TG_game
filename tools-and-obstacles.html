Charges, tools and the obstacles in your way

Charges are pieces you create on the board. Tools are the limited supplies below it. Knowing the difference helps you choose what to use and when.

Create and fire Pulse or Nova

Four matching tiles in a line, or five arranged in a T or L, create Pulse. Tap it to hit a 3×3 area. A line of five or more creates Nova, which hits its row and column. Firing either charge costs one move. Charges caught in another blast fire for free, allowing a chain reaction.

Move a charge only with a valid match

You can drag a charge into a neighbouring position if the ordinary tile moving the other way forms a valid match. This costs one move and does not fire the charge. A swap that only changes the charge’s position without making that match is rejected. Tap the charge when you want to fire it where it currently stands.

Tools spend stock, not moves

Laser hits one selected tile. Shuffle rearranges uncovered tiles. Swap exchanges two uncovered neighbours even without a match. Beam hits a selected row, and EMP hits a 3×3 area around a selected tile. Each use spends one item from the corresponding stock. Select the tool, then its target; Shuffle acts immediately. A tool’s blast can also activate a charge.

A cover breaks before its tile is collected

The tile under a protective cover is visible but cannot be swapped. Include it in a match of the same type to break the cover. The tile stays on the board and can be collected in a later match. A neighbouring match alone is not enough. Blasts also break covers; a covered tile struck by a tool loses its cover first.

Resonators need adjacent matches

A matched tile must touch a ring directly above, below, left or right. Each separate matching combination adds one charge to every ring it touches. Two independent combinations in the same wave can therefore add two charges to one ring. A connected T or L counts as one combination. Diagonal contact and direct blasts do not charge rings. Each ring shows its current charge and target.

A long cascade can leave a bonus Pulse

Three further matching cascades after the initial action can award one bonus Pulse on the board. It appears after the cascades settle, at most once per move; it does not fire automatically or enter your tool stock. Tap it later for one move. Tool actions do not award this bonus, and an already won puzzle does not need another charge.
