Privacy

Updated 18 October 2026 · playbeyondthesignal.com and game.playbeyondthesignal.com

Who operates the game

The controller is Michal Malich. For questions and personal data requests, email flambiz.cz@gmail.com.

Local saves and browser storage

The game stores guest and account copies of progress, stars, read stories, settings, energy, tool stock and sign-in session data in browser storage. The two addresses have separate storage. Clearing browser data can remove local saves; signing out does not delete your account. The standalone information pages store your language preference and do not start game analytics or Google sign-in.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud progress

Google sign-in is handled through Supabase. If you sign in, Supabase processes the account identifier, email and profile information provided by Google, together with your authentication session. Game progress, ratings and read log entries are saved against that account to let you continue on another device. Energy and tool stock are local, not shared cloud inventory. Providing sign-in data is necessary for account and cloud-save features; you can play as a guest.

Gameplay statistics

Only if you choose Allow statistics on first launch or in game Settings, we collect and send gameplay and visit records to Supabase under a random browser identifier: levels, results, attempts, moves, duration, goals, help used, activity times, device category and campaign parameters from the URL. These records are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. The gameplay report does not intentionally attach your Google name or email.

With statistics enabled, the game requests a country code from country.is. That service receives your IP address to determine the country. Only the country code is included in our game analytics. Hosting and service providers can also process IP addresses and request logs when delivering the service.

Advertisements and consent

The current game uses clearly marked test advertisements. They do not display paid advertising, and a simulated view does not generate advertising revenue. A Google AdSense account verification tag and ads.txt file are present; these alone do not load advertisements.

Before enabling real Google advertising, we will configure the applicable consent message and update this information for the integration being used. Google and advertising partners may then use cookies, browser storage, IP addresses and similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, personalise them. Advertising choices will be available through the consent message and its privacy settings. Use Advertising privacy choices in game Settings or the website footer to reopen the Google message. This requires a published and available message for this site. Unavailable consent data is never treated as acceptance. If you refuse the required advertising permissions, we do not start a rewarded ad; ordinary play remains available.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google’s advertising information.

An optional developer test link can load the Google H5 SDK after you request a reward. It uses Google test mode and displays a mock ad, not a paid rewarded placement. Loading the SDK contacts Google servers. Ordinary visits continue to use our local simulator.

Optional blind playtest

The separate playtest.html link offers an explicit test-data choice before play. It keeps a separate local order, level configurations and results, and sends attempts, moves, elapsed time, assistance, charge usage, difficulty ratings and optional notes under a separate random reporting identity. It does not use Google sign-in, ordinary game saves or ordinary statistics choices, and does not request country data or ads. Uploading can be paused in the test; local results remain available for export. Return in the same browser and device to resume. Test records use the same 90-day server retention. Local playtest records remain until browser storage is cleared; contact the controller for deletion and provide only the test session ID from the export, never private tokens.

Android app and age settings

The Android test app asks for an age group before enabling online features. It stores only the selected group on the device, not a date of birth. Players under 18 and those who prefer not to state an age play locally without Google sign-in, ad requests or outgoing gameplay statistics. Local progress remains on that device. Adults can choose optional Google cloud saves and statistics separately. Adult rewarded ads currently use Google demo units; Google Mobile Ads and UMP process ad requests and consent choices when those features are used. Age settings do not replace advertising consent.

Providers and purposes

GitHub Pages hosts the website; Supabase provides authentication, cloud saves and gameplay reporting; Google provides optional sign-in; country.is provides country lookup. Contact emails are handled through Gmail. Account data is used to provide the requested sign-in and saving service. Optional gameplay statistics are processed on the basis of your consent to improve difficulty and understand visits. You may refuse or withdraw in game Settings without losing play, rewards or cloud saves. Advertising consent is a separate choice. We do not sell your saves or contact details. Provider processing may involve locations outside your country; see GitHub, Supabase and Google for their privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until cleared, overwritten or reset. Optional local gameplay history is limited to 2,000 attempts and records older than 90 days are removed when the game is next opened. Turning statistics off clears local history and upload queues; turning them on never uploads pre-consent history. Cloud saves remain with your account until reset or removed following a verified request. Resetting progress is not account deletion. Detailed server gameplay and visit records expire after 90 days in a daily cleanup (up to one additional day); inactive reporting identities are also removed after 90 days once no retained records depend on them. Turn off and delete my statistics in game Settings requests deletion of this browser’s server records. Offline or unsuccessful requests stay pending and can be retried. Deletion does not affect game progress or delete your account. Other browsers or addresses have separate identities and must be handled there. A minimal revoked identifier and secret hash are retained solely to prevent old clients from recreating erased statistics; they are excluded from player counts. Service logs and backups follow the providers’ retention rules. For access, deletion or an objection to processing, contact the controller; we may need details to locate your account or browser records. Do not send session tokens.

Your choices and rights

You can use guest play, sign out, manage browser storage and request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data as applicable. You may object to processing and withdraw any consent previously given without affecting earlier processing. Contact the address above. You may also complain to your supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.
